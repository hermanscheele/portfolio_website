Research

Mathematics that touches real markets.

With Prof. Fred Espen Benth · University of Oslo

Constrained Kriging for Commodity Forward Curves

Reconstructing smooth, well-behaved forward price curves for crude oil (Brent & WTI) from a mix of liquid and illiquid (bid–ask) quotes. The method fits a Gaussian-process Kriging model, derives the conditional Gaussian distribution over unobserved maturities, and uses rejection sampling to enforce shape constraints and recover an average curve — extending toward constrained Kriging with data augmentation.

  • Gaussian processes
  • Kriging
  • Conditional Gaussians
  • Rejection sampling
  • Nelson–Siegel–Svensson
  • Python

Featured · Multi-agent AI

Dividend Reconciliation Agent System

A proof-of-concept multi-agent LLM system that automates dividend reconciliation between internal books and external custodians — cutting a manual process from ~60 minutes to ~5 minutes per run. Six specialized agents handle break detection (deterministic), market validation (LLM + web search), diagnosis, policy & tax-treaty compliance, auto-resolution with deterministic safeguards, and remediation drafting.
