Research

Mathematical research that utilizes energy and commodity markets data.

With Prof. Fred Espen Benth · BI

Constrained Kriging for Commodity Forward Curves

Oil-futures prices (Brent & WTI) are only quoted for a handful of dates — everything in between is thinly traded or missing. This project rebuilds the full, smooth price curve from those sparse, noisy quotes using a method called Kriging (a Gaussian process): it fills the gaps, puts an uncertainty band around every point, and keeps the curve shaped the way real oil markets actually behave.

  • Gaussian processes
  • Kriging
  • Conditional Gaussians
  • Rejection sampling
  • Nelson–Siegel–Svensson
  • Python
